Research

My research activity mostly focuses on applications of probability theory and stochastic analysis to fluid dynamics. I have been working in particular on invariant measures of fluid dynamical (S)PDEs, interacting particle systems (point vortex models) and their scaling limits. I have also been interested in limit theorems for local functionals of Gaussian fields, and more recently in disordered systems and matching problems.

All my publications are available on arXiv; see also my ORCiD page.
